Downloader Style Specimens

The same two screens — paste → resolve and active downloads — rendered in all six candidate directions. Identical content throughout, so the only variable is the aesthetic. Formats and delivery tags come from the real backend: Direct streams from the platform CDN, Server merge goes through ffmpeg.

  • Recommended
  • Viable
  • Advise against

These are static HTML specimens, not React Native. They exist to settle the visual direction before we write a single component. Colours are deliberately fixed per specimen — each is its own world, so none of them follow your system theme.

01

Modern Dark — Cinema Mobile

Recommended

Near-black rather than true black, hairline rgba(255,255,255,.08) borders, a single restrained indigo accent, and two slow ambient blobs behind everything. Thumbnails sit on a quiet ground, and the accent is spent in exactly one place — the primary action. Scores React Native 10/10 and Expo 10/10; nothing here fights the framework.

02

OLED Dark

Viable

True #000000 with #121212 cards and a single mint accent. Genuinely excellent for battery and contrast, and the simplest of the six to build and maintain. The cost is character: side by side with 01 it reads correct but anonymous — there is nothing here a user would describe to a friend.

Watch: pure #000000 smears on OLED panels during fast scrolling. If you pick this, lift the base to #050505 and keep the neon accent — which is essentially direction 01 with less atmosphere.

03

Glassmorphism

Viable as accent

Frosted translucent panels over a saturated blurred ground. Genuinely striking on the hero moment, and the format sheet is the perfect place for it. As a whole-app system it struggles: text contrast shifts depending on which blob sits behind it, so a row that is legible at the top of the list can fail near the bottom.

  • #1B1140 ground
  • #F0508A blob
  • #3E7BFA blob
  • #23C8A8 blob
  • surface rgba(255,255,255,.11)
  • blur 16 · radius 20

Cost: every blurred surface is a real GPU pass. Six BlurViews stacked over a scrolling list is exactly where React Native drops frames — and this app scrolls a list while animating progress bars. Use it for the header and format sheet, not the rows.

04

Cyberpunk / Neon Terminal

Advise against

Matrix green on near-black, magenta accents, monospace throughout, scanline overlay. It is the most immediately distinctive of the six and the most fun to build — and it is the one you will regret. Monospace at 12px destroys scanning speed in a list, which is the screen users live in.

  • #0D0D0D bg
  • #00FF66 text
  • #FF00E5 accent
  • #00FFF0 info
  • radius 0 · Share Tech Mono
Home · paste → resolve
Downloads · progress

The real problem is not contrast. #00FF66 on #0D0D0D measures about 14:1 — it passes AAA on paper. Saturated green on black causes halation: the glyph edges bloom and the eye cannot settle, which is why terminals default to dimmed green. Add the scanline overlay and you have a screen nobody reads for thirty seconds, let alone monitors a download in.

05

Material You — MD3

Safe

Real MD3 tokens: #6750A4 primary, tonal #EADDFF containers, pill CTA, Roboto. Native on Android, well documented, and free accessibility. But a media app in light mode fights its own content — the thumbnails now shout against a near-white ground instead of sitting in it, which is the opposite of what you want.

06

Claymorphism

Advise against

Soft pastel ground, puffy dual shadows, oversized 26px radii, chunky Nunito. Warm and friendly in isolation. The trouble shows up at exactly the density this app needs: every card carries two large shadows, so four download rows produce eight overlapping blurs and the list turns to mush.

  • #E9E6F7 ground
  • #EFEDFB surface
  • #4B4470 text
  • #9B95BE muted
  • #B9A6F0 accent
  • radius 26 · Nunito
Home · paste → resolve
Downloads · progress

Measured failure: muted text #9B95BE on #EFEDFB is roughly 2.3:1 — well under the 4.5:1 minimum. Every file size, speed readout and status line on these screens is unreadable, and darkening the text far enough to pass breaks the soft look that motivates the style in the first place.

Seeing them together makes the case better than the table did: 01 Modern Dark is the one to build. It gives the thumbnails a quiet ground, spends its accent once, and its ambient motion is cheap enough to run alongside animated progress bars. Borrow the frosted header from 03, and keep 05 in reserve if Android-native feel ever matters more than identity.
